Pregnancy Basics

Pregnancy is divided into three trimesters. Each stage brings new changes for both mother and baby. Focus on what matters right now — regular checkups, balanced nutrition, and gentle movement.

Nutrition

A varied diet with plenty of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins supports healthy pregnancy. Stay hydrated. Avoid alcohol and limit caffeine. Consult your doctor about supplements.

Wellbeing

Rest when you need it. Light walking, stretching, and breathing exercises can help. Talk to someone you trust — emotional health matters as much as physical health.

Preparation for Birth

Learn about labor signs, pack a bag with essentials, and know your route to the hospital. Attend prenatal classes if available. Having a plan helps you feel calm and ready.
